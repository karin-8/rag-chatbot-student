# From Colab to Code: RAG Chatbot Workshop

Turn your Week 5 RAG chatbot from a Colab notebook into a real, runnable
local application: version-controlled, modular, tested, with a persistent
vector database.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`student-guide/`](student-guide/index.html) | The step-by-step workshop guide. Download and open `index.html` in your browser. |
| [`rag-chatbot-student/`](rag-chatbot-student/) | The starter project you'll work in. |
| [`rag-chatbot-solution/`](rag-chatbot-solution/) | A finished version of the in-class build, for checking your work. |

## Getting started

1. Click **Code → Download ZIP** and unzip it. Use Download ZIP rather than
   `git clone`: during the workshop you'll turn `rag-chatbot-student/` into
   your own git repository, starting from an empty history.
2. Open `student-guide/index.html` in your browser and complete the
   **Appendix A: Before class** at home. It downloads about 1.1 GB of model
   weights, so do it on a good connection.
